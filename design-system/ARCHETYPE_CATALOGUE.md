# Aravest Archetype Catalogue
**36 communication archetypes across 8 families, mapped to 18 technical layouts (T01–T18).**
Each entry: purpose · audience (B Board / I Investor / C Investment Committee / M Management) · content type · maximum content · card configuration · colour mode · permitted elements · sequencing (before → after) · correct / incorrect use.

Technical layouts: T01 cover-dark · T02 cover-image · T03 divider · T04 statement · T05 agenda · T06 card framework · T07 twin cards · T08 anchor dashboard · T09 KPI+chart · T10 chart+rail · T11 institutional table · T12 row cards · T13 decision-light · T14 dark emphasis · T15 process · T16 case study · T17 text+rail · T18 appendix.

---

## Family 1 · Editorial narrative

**1.1 Board cover** — T01 · B,C,M · deck identity · title ≤5 words + 1-sentence subtitle · no cards · dark-field · Spectral title (roman+italic), gold hairline, vertical image crop, tonal facet · opens deck → agenda/statement · ✓ every Board/IC paper ✗ investor road-shows (use 1.2).
**1.2 Investor cover** — T02 · I · deck identity · as 1.1 · no cards · dark-field (image+66% navy overlay) · full-bleed photography · opens deck → agenda · ✓ corporate introductions, fundraising ✗ internal papers.
**1.3 Section divider** — T03 · all · transition · numeral + ≤6-word title + optional 1-line note · no cards · dark-field · Spectral numeral/title, short gold rule · between sections → any white slide · ✓ 3–6 per long deck ✗ consecutive dividers, ✗ after a dark slide.
**1.4 Strategic statement** — T04 · B,I · single proposition · ≤30 words + optional 1-sentence support · no cards · white · Spectral 30pt with one italic phrase, gold rule · after cover/divider → evidence slide · ✓ one per section max ✗ as a routine text slide.
**1.5 Pull quote** — T04 variant · I,B · voice/conviction · ≤30 words + attribution · no cards · white · Spectral italic, thin rule · after evidence → next topic · ✓ genuine quotations ✗ invented aphorisms.
**1.6 Agenda / contents** — T05 · all · orientation · ≤7 items + optional sub-notes · no cards · white · serif numerals, hairlines · after cover → statement/divider · ✓ decks >12 slides ✗ sub-agendas per section (use divider note).

## Family 2 · Modular card frameworks

**2.1 Four-element progression** — T06 · B,I · four linked conclusions · 4 cards, each title + ≤2 short sentences (13pt) · 1×4 supporting cards (accents only where meaning: e.g. teal on the record card, steel on the pathways card) + primary conclusion band · white+tints · ✓ executive summary ✗ four unrelated bullets in card costume.
**2.2 Three pillars** — T06 1×3 · B,I,C · values/framework · 3 cards ≤35 words each + principle band · equal white cards, graphite borders; primary band Deep Navy · white · ✓ The Aravest Way ✗ colouring each pillar differently without meaning.
**2.3 One primary + two supporting** — T08 skeleton · B,M · one governing figure/message + context · primary Deep Navy card + 2 white cards · white · ✓ KPI-led stories ✗ three equal tiles.
**2.4 Two unequal cards** — T07 asymmetric (60/40) · B,C · main point + qualifier · 2 cards · white+one tint · ✓ proposal + risk note ✗ hiding the qualifier in the small card.
**2.5 2×2 grid** — T13 skeleton · B,C,M · four parallel items · 4 compact cards ≤3 lines each · white cards, graphite borders · ✓ decisions, four workstreams ✗ 2×2 of unrelated content.
**2.6 Staggered emphasis card** — T06 with one emphasised card (tint+accent rule, e.g. the 2027–29 phase) · all · sequence with a highlighted stage · ✓ timeline emphasis ✗ emphasising more than one card.
**2.7 Dark anchor + light supports** — T08 · B,I · a number/claim that governs the slide · Deep Navy anchor + 2–3 supporting/utility · ✓ sponsor capital ✗ two dark anchors on one slide.

## Family 3 · Comparison & pathways

**3.1 Two-pathway strategy** — T07 · B,I,C · the two-pathway model · 5 aligned rows per card, values 13pt · twin equal cards, teal/steel top rules + 7% tints + navy connector band · white+tints · ✓ the canonical pathways slide ✗ any asymmetry implying preference.
**3.2 Balanced comparison** — T07 · all · any A/B with equal status · ≤5 aligned rows · twin cards (accents only if entities carry pathway meaning; otherwise graphite) · ✓ options analysis ✗ using teal/steel for non-pathway entities.
**3.3 Comparison matrix** — T11 two-value columns · C,B · attribute-level comparison ≥5 attributes · table, no cards · white · marker column optional · ✓ recycling routes ✗ matrices of prose paragraphs.
**3.4 Current vs future** — T07 · B,M · state transition · left neutral "today", right tinted "target" + connector stating the shift · ✓ operating-model change ✗ colouring "today" red.
**3.5 With / without (cause-effect)** — T07 · B,C · consequence of a capability · 2 cards: neutral vs teal-tinted chain (→ sequences) + conclusion band · ✓ warehousing ✗ fear-framing the "without" card.
**3.6 Scenario comparison** — T07 1×3 narrow cards or T11 · C,B · 2–4 scenarios · per scenario ≤30 words or table rows · neutral cards; accents only for pathway-linked scenarios · ✓ sensitivity discussion ✗ implied forecasts without basis notes.
**3.7 Options & recommendation** — T12 rows + T13 principle band · B · options with one recommended · rows neutral, recommended row teal/steel-tinted + "RECOMMENDED" tag; rationale in band · ✓ Board option papers ✗ gold on the recommended row.

## Family 4 · Financial & data

**4.1 Anchor dashboard** — T08 · B,M,I · one anchor figure + two profiles · anchor card + 2 chart cards + utility band; equation as typography between hairlines · ✓ capital & AUM ✗ four equal KPI tiles (banned default).
**4.2 KPI strip + chart** — T09 · M,B · several measures + one trend · ≤4 KPIs (typographic strip, hairline separators — not cards) + one flat chart · ✓ management reporting ✗ KPI tiles with icons.
**4.3 Chart + commentary rail** — T10 · all · one chart that needs interpretation · chart 60–65% + utility rail card ≤3 observations · ✓ trend discussions ✗ rail repeating axis values.
**4.4 Financial bridge / waterfall** — T09 frame, bridge bars (start/end Navy, increases teal, decreases graphite-tint, subtotal rules) · C,B · movement decomposition · ≤8 elements, labels direct · ✓ NAV/AUM bridges ✗ rainbow waterfall.
**4.5 AUM movement** — 4.4 specialised to the identity Closing = Opening + New − Realisations ± Val/FX · M,B,I.
**4.6 Actual vs budget** — T11 numeric table (Actual/Budget/Var columns, negatives in parentheses, right-aligned) + one takeaway band · M,B · ✗ red/green variance colouring — use parentheses and one teal/steel highlight.
**4.7 Trend with scenario range** — T10 with banded range (38% tint band, solid line) · C,B · ranges, always "illustrative/not a forecast" footer · ✗ unlabelled fan charts.
**4.8 Analytical dashboard** — T08 extended (anchor + up to 3 supporting cards) · M · densest permitted data slide; 13pt floor still applies · ✗ more than 4 data modules per slide.

**8.2 Matters for endorsement vs noting** — T07 twin (endorse teal-tinted, noting neutral) + T13 furniture · B · ✗ burying decisions among noting items.
**8.3 Recommendation & rationale** — T17 (recommendation as bold lead) + rail of rationale + principle band · B,C.
**8.4 Governing principle** — full-width Deep Navy card, label + 13pt white text · B,C · one per deck section max · ✗ demoting it to a grey box.
**8.5 Dark-field decision / synthesis** — T14 · B · the pivotal moment or closing proposition · one tonal card, 15–16pt white, pathway phrase pair in light teal/steel · **once per deck** · ✗ routine content on dark fields.
**8.6 Key takeaways** — T06 1×3 (three message cards + navy band) · all · closing a section · ✗ more than four takeaways.
**8.7 Next steps** — T12 rows (action / owner / timing columns) · B,M · ≤5 rows · ✗ vague actions without owners.
**8.8 Closing slide** — fixed: Deep Navy, centred logo, verbatim registered address, confidentiality · all decks end here.

### Sequencing quick-matrix (what follows well)
- After **divider**: statement, twin cards, process — never another dark slide.
- After **twin cards**: table, chart+rail, case study — not another twin.
- After **table**: card framework, case study, statement — not another table.
- After **dark emphasis**: appendix/divider/closing only.
- **Decision** sits after the evidence chain (dashboard/table/case), before appendix.
