# Aravest Deck — Author Quick Reference (one page)

**Start from** `Aravest_Master_Template.pptx` — duplicate the layout slide you need (T01–T18). Check `ARCHETYPE_CATALOGUE.md` if unsure which.

## Do
- **Headlines state the conclusion**, sentence case, ≤2 lines at 24pt (Board mode).
- Body **≥13pt** Aptos Graphite (tables may go to 12pt; appendix 11–12pt). Footnotes 8.5–9pt Slate.
- **Teal = Pathway 1 (builds the record) · Steel = Pathway 2 (accelerates scale).** Use them only with that meaning; equal weight always.
- Cards: ~8pt rounded corners, 0.75pt tonal border, 7% tint, the one standard shadow. 2–4 meaningful cards per slide, three tiers (Deep Navy primary / white-tint supporting / graphite-tint utility).
- Dense tables (≥5 rows): **3pt pathway marker bars, no row tints**, no alternating fills.
- Deep Navy for: conclusion bands, governing principles, the KPI anchor, dark-field moments.
- Every schematic/projection: *"Illustrative profile only — not a forecast."* Every external figure: a source line.
- Vary composition slide-to-slide; flip through the sorter before circulating.

## Don't
- ❌ Gold anywhere except: cover hairline, divider rule, the single "For decision" marker.
- ❌ More than two accent colours on a working slide; no gradients, bevels, glows, heavy shadows, dark outlines.
- ❌ The same composition (or card count) more than twice in a row; no eyebrow on every slide.
- ❌ Four equal KPI tiles; icons-per-bullet; pies >6 segments; red/green status colouring.
- ❌ Spectral for body/tables/labels — covers, dividers, pull quotes and one headline phrase only (≤10% of text).
- ❌ Dark-field slides for routine content — covers, dividers, the decision, the closing synthesis only (~10% of deck).
- ❌ Touching disclaimer or entity wording — Legal owns it; use the preserved slides as-is.

## Fixed furniture (never move)
Logo top-right · "Private and Confidential" bottom-left · page number bottom-right · margins 0.7″ (editorial 1.0″) · footer baseline 7.06″.

## Colour codes
Navy `#002B5C` · Deep Navy `#002147` · Graphite `#35464F` · Slate `#869397` · Teal `#008080` · Steel `#4682B4` · Gold `#C8A951` · Mist `#E8E8E8` · tints: pathway 7%, utility 4.5%, borders 40%/25%.

## Modes
**Board/IC (default):** 24pt titles, 13–13.5pt body, dense, Cover T01, light decision slide.
**Investor:** 30–36pt titles, 15–18pt body, more whitespace, Cover T02, imagery prominent.
**Dark decision variant (T14):** emphasis only — once per deck.
