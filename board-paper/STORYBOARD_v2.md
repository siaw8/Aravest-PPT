# Aravest "Next Phase" — Board Discussion Paper · Rebuilt Storyboard (for approval)

**Method.** Three independent storyboards (narrative-flow / visual-variety / message-hierarchy) were
built from the locked v8 narrative and scored by a critic panel against the eight rebuild
requirements. The narrative-flow concept won (37 vs 32/32) and is the backbone; three grafts from the
other two fix its only weaknesses. No slides are built yet.

**Shape.** A **15-slide discussion body** (down from 22), each carrying *one* message with prose moved
to speaker notes — followed by a **7-page reference appendix** (the dense tables the Board should not
be walked through) and the verbatim disclaimer + closing. Total file ≈ 25, but the *presented* deck is
15. The two-pathway model is the visual spine; the argument runs in four movements.

## Disposition (what moved, and where nothing is lost)

**Merged**
- S1 + S2 → slide 2 (purpose statement sits over the 2025→2030 timeline — one opener, not two).
- S3 + S4 → slide 3 (first-principle headline + the three constant values + deployment rail).

**Moved to appendix** (dense reference tables, off the discussion body)
- Investment philosophy (5) · evidence criteria (6) · recycling routes (6) · full pathway matrix (5) ·
  value-creation playbooks (6) · structuring standards (6) · AUM identity & methodology.

**Moved to speaker notes** (supporting prose the presenter speaks, not shown)
- "Why the Aravest Way matters" (consistency / trust / repeatability) · the four deployment tests ·
  Pathway 1 & 2 capital/risk mechanics · Conrad names (GIC, M&G) + "not an assured timetable" ·
  pipeline names/figures-refresh caveat + private-credit (SMDAM) exclusion · AUM "Finance to populate"
  caveat · "exceptions must be explicit and separately approved" elaboration.

**Deleted:** nothing. Every element of the locked narrative survives on the face, in the appendix, or
in notes — the strategy is not reopened.

## Layout-family & rhythm check
- **Editorial:** 1, 2, 3, 15 · **Diagram/process:** 4, 6, 8, 14 (only one is a left→right ribbon; 6 is a
  loop, 8 a two-state chain, 14 a node flow — no ribbon repeats, none consecutive) · **Comparison:** 5, 7 ·
  **Financial:** 9 · **Case study:** 10 · **Cards/rows:** 11, 12 · **Governing band:** 13.
- **Cards used on 5 of 15** body slides (hero+pillars, twin, balanced-2, rows, staggered bands) — never
  a repeated grid; 10 slides carry no cards.
- **Colour rhythm:** ~70% white, tinted only 6-7 and 12 (never 3 tinted consecutively), dark only 1 / 15 / 17.
- **Sequencing:** twin-pathway (5) is the single largest-headline moment; the dark synthesis (15) is
  followed only by the disclaimer and closing.

## One open choice
The body is **divider-free** for leanness — movements are signalled in each opener's eyebrow
("II · The record we need"). If you'd prefer explicit **dark section dividers** at the four movements,
that adds ~3 slides. Recommended: keep it divider-free; add only the appendix divider.
