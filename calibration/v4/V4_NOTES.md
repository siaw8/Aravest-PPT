# Calibration Set v4 — functional colour + institutional card language

Content is unchanged (verbatim from the Locked Board Strategy Narrative v8). V4 applies the
approved colour roles and reintroduces cards as a core, disciplined component.

## Colour roles as implemented
| Colour | Role | Where it appears in v4 |
|---|---|---|

The connector on the two-pathway slide states the visual language literally:
**"Pathway 1 builds the record"** set in light teal; **"Pathway 2 can accelerate scale"** in light
steel — on a Deep Navy field, with equal weight.

**Guardrails held:** max two active accents per slide (teal+steel where both pathways appear;
otherwise fewer) · tints at ~5% (graphite utility ~4.5%) · no gradients or bevels · gold nowhere in
working hierarchy.

## Card system
- **Form:** rounded corners ≈ 8pt radius (0.09"); generous internal padding (~0.24"); reduced
  inter-card gaps (0.15–0.16").
- **Borders/shadow:** 0.75pt tonal borders (35% accent tint or 25% graphite tint); one consistent
  subtle shadow spec — Deep Navy at 16% alpha, 2pt offset, 5pt blur. Dark-field cards carry no shadow.
- **Hierarchy:** **Primary** = Deep Navy fill, white text (sponsor capital, governing principle,
  conclusion bands) · **Supporting** = white or 5% pathway tint (strategic cards, pathway cards,
  decision cards, pipeline rows) · **Utility** = 4.5% graphite tint, no shadow (Conrad proof band).
- **Density:** 3–5 meaningful cards per slide; footnotes, sources, equation and column headers stay
  typographic — not everything is boxed.

## Six distinct compositions (calibration requirement)
1. **Exec summary** — editorial headline → four differentiated strategic cards → full-width navy band.
2. **Two pathways** — twin equal tall pathway cards (teal/steel) → navy parallel connector.
3. **Capital & AUM** — dark anchor card + two chart cards + utility proof band (analytical dashboard).
4. **Board decision (light)** — 2×2 compact decision cards → navy governing-principle card.
5. **Board decision (dark-field alternative)** — Deep Navy field, tonal cards, single gold marker.
6. **Pipeline** — three horizontal row cards with pathway markers and whisper tints.

## Deck rhythm (against the 70/20/10 target)
Covers (2) and the dark decision alternative are dark-field; exec summary / decision (light) are
predominantly white with one navy band; pathways / AUM / pipeline carry meaningful light tints.
Across a full 20–30-slide deck the system targets ~70% white · ~20% tinted-structure · ~10% dark-field.
